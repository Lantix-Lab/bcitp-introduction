## Name and definition

**BrainExpanse** is the name of a modular computational platform for brain science and brain-computer interface theoretical research.

**Full English name:** Digital Twin Brain based Brain Computer Interface Theoretical Platform<br>
**中文名称：** 数字孪生脑-脑机接口理论平台

BrainExpanse organizes multimodal neuroimaging processing, individualized digital twin brain modeling, virtual physical field simulation, brain-region-scale mapping, and scientific visualization into one computational research chain. Its purpose is to support theoretical reasoning, mechanism exploration, and candidate intervention comparison before subsequent real-world experiments.

## Conceptual scope

```text
Multimodal neuroimaging data
  -> PRE: neuroimaging preprocessing
  -> DTB: digital twin brain modeling
  -> VPF: virtual physical field simulation
  -> EVA: virtual intervention evaluation (under development)
  -> visualization and result review
```

The central idea is to connect two scales that are often treated separately:

1. **Individualized brain-network dynamics** — structural connectivity, functional connectivity, and neural dynamics are used to build a computable digital representation of an individual brain.
2. **External physical stimulation** — electrical, magnetic, optical, and related stimulation are modeled in individualized head tissues and mapped from continuous or mesh-based fields to atlas and ROI scales.

The resulting chain supports systematic comparison of candidate stimulation settings, model parameters, and network-level responses. It is intended to inform research design; it does not replace real experiments, clinical judgment, or regulatory evaluation.

## Current development boundary

- PRE, DTB, VPF, and UTL form the current technical foundation of BrainExpanse.
- EVA remains under development and is not yet a complete research or validation module.
- The algorithm implementation is a non-public, actively developed project. This repository publishes the platform introduction, concept definition, and public communication materials only.
